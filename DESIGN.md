# Cairose: proposed visual direction

This describes the reviewable prototype, not an approved production brand system.

## Context

A florist owner explores Cairose on her phone between orders, in a bright shop, and needs to see whether it could work for her customers. Use light, legible surfaces and clear actions.

## Brand surfaces

Deep botanical green, warm yellow, and clean warm neutrals. Use green as a substantial brand surface, not small decorative accents. Large, direct type paired with restrained serif emphasis. Existing floral photographs provide context; the connected plan experience provides evidence. Preserve the Cairose wordmark.

## Product surfaces

Light workspace, compact sans-serif hierarchy, predictable navigation, date-led delivery lists. Color identifies active controls and meaningful status. Avoid large decorative metrics or empty dashboard areas.

## Prototype scope

A separate static route at `/propuesta/` demonstrates the landing, storefront, step-by-step plan creation, confirmation, customer plan management, florist agenda, plan list, catalog editing, brand setup, and contact handoff. All mutations are in-memory simulations. No real subscriptions, payments, messages, or backend changes are made.

## Assets and limits

Use the existing project images and mark them as demonstration content. They are provisional for production art direction; commissioned florist photography would make the brand more specific. The contact number must be supplied before a direct WhatsApp handoff is enabled. No fake phone number.
